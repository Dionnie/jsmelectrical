Logo set

JSM Electrical logo

The logo in its original black and grey, with a version for every place it appears: light and dark backgrounds, one-colour jobs, small spaces, browser tabs, phone home screens and social profiles. Every file is in docs/assets/logo/, as SVG (sharp at any size) and PNG.

Colours

Two colours, as on the ute door: black for the letters, grey for the J's tick and the M's diagonal. The grey never moves to other parts.

Space and size

Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the J's grey tick (about 15% of the logo's height) on every side. Nothing else goes inside it.

  • Full logo: at least 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print. Below that, "ELECTRICAL" gets too thin.
  • Monogram: at least 60 px wide, 15 mm in print.
  • Smaller than that: use the J icon.

Don't

  • Change the grey, or move it to other letters.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or recreate "ELECTRICAL" in another font.
  • Put the two-colour logo on a mid-grey background, where the grey disappears. Use a one-colour version.
  • Place it on a busy photo without a dark overlay behind it.
  • Use the old 189 × 80 PNG anywhere it's shown larger than that; it goes blurry.
