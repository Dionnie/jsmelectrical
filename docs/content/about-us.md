**JSM ELECTRICAL** is an Electrical Maintenance and Service Repairs company that services the surrounding areas of Geelong and Portarlington as well as technical projects across rural and remote Australia.

**Founded in 2023**

We are team of highly qualified and experienced electrical professionals, that quickly made a name for ourselves in the industries of residential, commercial and industrial electrical service repairs and maintenance as our expertise has allowed us to quickly take on and finish a diverse amount of work on cabling, wiring, lighting, engineering, design and technical manpower for large-scale projects.

In the challenging and tough climate of regional Australia, as we have completed electrical and underground cabling and wiring projects when other companies have found it too remote or too challenging.

Whether your home or your business needs electrical troubleshooting, installations, or preventive maintenance, we’ve got you covered.
