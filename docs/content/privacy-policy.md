## Privacy Policy

**Privacy Policy for JSM Electrical**

- Introduction
JSM Electrical (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website: [https://jsmelectrical.com.au](https://jsmelectrical.com.au) (the “Website”) and use our services.

- Collection of Personal Information
We collect personal information when you voluntarily provide it to us through our Website, specifically through our enquiry contact form. The information we collect may include:

* Name: To identify you and personalize our communications.

* Contact Details: Such as phone number and email address, to respond to your inquiries.

- Use of Personal Information
We use your personal information for the following purposes:

* To respond to your inquiries and provide you with information about our electrical services.

* To communicate with you regarding your requests and provide customer support.

- Google Analytics
We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about how visitors use our Website. This information helps us understand website traffic and improve our Website’s performance. Google Analytics collects data such as:

* Website traffic patterns.

* Pages visited.

* Time spent on the Website.

* General demographic information.

This data is used for analytical purposes only and does not identify individual users. You can review Google’s privacy policy here: [Insert Link to Google Analytics Privacy Policy].

- Disclosure of Personal Information
We will not disclose your personal information to third parties except in the following circumstances:

* When required by law.

* To protect our rights or property.

* In the event of a business transfer.

- Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

- Access and Correction
You have the right to access and correct your personal information that we hold. If you would like to access or correct your information, please contact us using the contact details provided below.

- Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our Website. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

- Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

* jack@jsmelectrical.com.au

* [https://jsmelectrical.com.au](https://jsmelectrical.com.au/)

- Australian Privacy Principles
JSM Electrical is committed to complying with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) as outlined in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).
