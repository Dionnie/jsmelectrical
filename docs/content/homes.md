## Electrical Services & Maintenance for Homes & Residential Areas

**At JSM Electrical**, we understand that your home is your living space. That’s why we offer a comprehensive range of electrical services designed to keep your living space safe, comfortable, and efficient in completing our job. Whether you need repairs, maintenance, or installations for your existing home or for a planned renovation, our team of skilled electricians are here to assist you.

We diagnose and repair various electrical issues, such as faulty wiring, blown fuses, and tripped circuit breakers.

**Maintenance**

Our regular maintenance services help prevent electrical problems and ensure optimal performance of your home’s electrical systems.

**Upgrades and Modernisations**

We can upgrade your home’s electrical system to accommodate new appliances, improve energy efficiency, or enhance your home’s aesthetic appeal.

**New Home Wiring**

Our electricians specialise in wiring new homes, ensuring that all electrical systems comply with safety codes and meet your specific requirements.

**Home Renovation**

Whether you’re remodeling your kitchen, bathroom, or adding a new room, or a total home rebuild project we can provide the necessary electrical services from mapping your wiring, electrical engineering and installation to support your home makeover project.
