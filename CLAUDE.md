# JSM Electrical: WordPress on Laragon

We are the Global Webforce team. We built JSM's live site, and this project covers:
- the WordPress revamp of that site;
- a knowledge base on JSM: the business, its website, and its social media and other presence online (`docs/`);
- the discussion with JSM, and the proposal (`docs/ui-ux-proposal.md`).

We also offer the digital services JSM don't use yet (social media, digital marketing). Client documents speak as the Global Webforce team ("we", "our team") and never name an individual. They own the current site and its faults as our work, and can suggest those services where they'd help.

## Files

- `docs/company.md`: business and content facts. Read it before writing site copy or pages.
- `docs/content/`: the live site's copy (2026-10-21, updated after our fixes on 2026-10-22), one file per page plus `site-wide.md` for the header and footer. Read only the page you're revamping.
- `docs/facebook.md`, `docs/instagram.md`: what JSM's Facebook and Instagram showed (2026-10-22): reviews, REC number, team, about 20 dated jobs, partner builders. JSM run both themselves, and Facebook is out of date: confirm before using either on the site.
- `docs/ui-ux-proposal.md`: the proposal for JSM, with the questions to ask them. Images: `docs/proposal/`.
- `docs/logo.html`: the logo set (black and grey; reversed, one-colour, monogram, favicon, app icon, social profile), with its files in `docs/assets/logo/`.
- `docs/design.md`: the revamp's design system (tokens, motion, components, page plan). Read it before building theme styles or templates. Prototype: `docs/design.html`.
